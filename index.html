Selected Research

Preserving useful structure when one modality becomes unreliable, and recovering consistent images when both fail.

Active RGB–NIR Co-Failure Completion

When RGB and NIR Fail Together: Completing the Blind Spot of Active NIR Illumination

Sep. 2026–Present First author

Advisor: Prof. Guoyu Lu (Indiana University Bloomington)

  • Developed a geometry-conditioned diffusion framework for RGB–NIR co-failure, jointly generating stereo views across consecutive frames with aligned noise and training on real failure transplants and geo-registered daytime captures.
  • Reduced co-failure depth AbsRel by 38% (IGEV++) and 24% (RAFT-Stereo) relative to the strongest tested baselines, without downstream fine-tuning or LiDAR input at inference.

RGB–NIR Fusion

LaminaFuse: Overexposure-Robust RGB–NIR Fusion via Local Contrast

Nov. 2025–Sep. 2026 First author Unpublished manuscript

Advisor: Prof. Guoyu Lu (Indiana University Bloomington)

Code
  • Developed a self-supervised RGB–active NIR fusion method that combines log-domain local contrast using an analytic contrast-energy prior and learned weight corrections, preserving visible structure under NIR overexposure.
  • Achieved a visible-edge transfer score of 0.711 versus 0.371 for the strongest tested fusion baseline in high-NIR regions on MS2, without fine-tuning.

Research Direction

I am interested in human-centered visual perception, particularly interactive perceptual calibration and image reconstruction from ordinary RGB images. I hope to connect user-guided visual matching with interpretable models of how images are perceived.

In the longer term, I aim to study spectral imaging and physics-based image formation, examining how illumination, material properties, and scene geometry jointly shape captured images and human visual experience.
